Speech Key 0.2.3

  • Cleaner text, same words. Speech Key now also drops words said twice in a row (“should should”) and the cut-off start of a word (“w white paper”). It never rewrites what you said.
  • See and copy the raw words. In History, a row the cleanup changed has a Raw switch: it shows exactly what was heard, and a click copies that instead.
  • Say a name, then spell it (“Sara S-A-R-A-H”) and Speech Key writes it that way from then on. Undo it in History.
  • The pill always shows up. After days of running it could go missing over full-screen apps. It now checks that it is really on screen.
  • Fixed a crash that could hit during a long dictation or a meeting snapshot.
  • Fewer “press ⌘V” misses. Slow apps get more time to take the paste.
  • Meetings without computer audio (a phone on speaker, people in a room) are treated as normal: voices are labeled “Room”.
  • Names: a glossary name no longer replaces ordinary words that only sound like it.
