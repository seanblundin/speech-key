Speech Key 0.2.4

  • Speech Key is in your Dock now, with the dot that shows it is running. Click it to open History, Meetings and Glossary. The menu bar icon still works, and Speech Key tells you if a full menu bar is hiding it.
  • A clearer first launch. The speech model starts downloading right away, and the setup window shows how far along it is.
  • Talking to the agent (hold ⇧ and the globe key) works much better. It now knows what you just dictated, so “that last one should be Priya” fixes the right word.
  • Copy and paste work in the Glossary and History fields.
  • Cleanup no longer drops a word from sentences like “the last thing I did, did you catch that?”
