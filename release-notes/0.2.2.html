Speech Key 0.2.2

  • Updates itself. From now on, new versions arrive on their own.
  • “Pasted” means pasted. If no app takes the text, Speech Key leaves it on your clipboard and says “press ⌘V”.
  • A new menu-bar panel: History, Meetings and Glossary tabs. Meetings get titles and one-line summaries, written on your Mac.
  • Glossary table you can edit, sort by Recent, Most used or A–Z, and fixes that only apply in context (“only in book club talk”).
  • Faster and steadier: the pill shows up in about a quarter of a second, even after a burst of key presses. Hands-free keeps its timer, and one tap ends it cleanly.
  • Speech Key is now open source: github.com/seanblundin/speech-key.
